Veteran Life
After the Uniform

A 3D life sim about the first months after service. Walk the neighborhood, work shifts, keep your needs up, and build a civilian life one day at a time. Entertainment first. Your claim moves in the background while you live your life.

Backgrounds change your starting skills, cash, and story beats, so a second life plays differently. In game flavor only, never a real world label.

Move: virtual joystick (phone), WASD or arrow keys (desktop), or tap the ground to walk there.
Camera: drag anywhere to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
Music: Maple Street Radio 98.5 plays three original tracks in rotation once your day starts. The speaker button at the top right mutes it fast, and Home and More has the on and off switch plus a volume slider.
Live: walk into home, the VFW Hall, IronWorks Gym, Dot's Diner, and the Neon Palm Social Club. Rooms are real: walk to a glowing ring inside to eat, train, dance, or rest. Talk to neighbors, they remember you. Watch your five needs, work shifts, pay the weekly bills, and climb the Veteran 500.
Guide: the Resource Guide lists real veteran programs with official links, kept clearly separate from the fictional game.
Online: tap the Offline chip at the top any time to open the multiplayer lobby. Pick a display name, join the one shared room, and you will see other players walking the same streets with name labels, plus a simple room chat. No account, no email, display name only. Solo play works fully offline.
Hide the HUD: tap Hide HUD at the top right (or press H on desktop) to hide every panel and button and see only the world. Tap Show HUD or press H again and everything returns exactly as it was. The game and multiplayer keep running while it is hidden. The camera button beside it is Photo Mode.
My Life: the transition checklist, friendships, achievements, daily quests, and your story ending. Home and More: decor, housing upgrades, vehicles and Eastside travel, your small business, wardrobe, sound, and accessibility options.
